I'm a final-year undergraduate student (Level 4, Term 2) in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), majoring in Electronics and Photonics. My research centers on post-CMOS hardware — I'm especially drawn to how two-dimensional materials and spintronic device physics can be combined to build energy-efficient, hardware-intrinsic primitives for neuromorphic computing. My undergraduate thesis, supervised by Dr. Orchi Hassan, designs a hybrid spintronic-CMOS pulse-width-modulation generator built around a spin-orbit-torque oscillator, work that has deepened my interest in device-to-circuit co-design.

Alongside this, I've built a broad foundation in VLSI design, analog integrated circuits, and embedded systems through coursework, laboratory projects, and an industrial IC-design attachment — you can find a selection of these on my [projects](/projects/) page, and my full academic background on my [CV](/cv/) page.

I'm now seeking PhD positions in the United States, where I hope to join a research group exploring novel materials and device physics for next-generation computing hardware, while growing as a teacher-researcher.
