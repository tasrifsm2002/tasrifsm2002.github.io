I am an electrical and electronic engineering researcher interested in spintronic devices, emerging materials, and energy-efficient computing architectures. I am a lecturer in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at BRAC University and an M.Sc. student at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), where I also completed my B.Sc. with a CGPA of 3.94/4.00 (top 6% of the class).

My undergraduate thesis, supervised by Dr. Orchi Hassan, designed a hybrid spintronic-CMOS pulse-width-modulation generator built around a spin Hall nano-oscillator (7.5 GHz oscillation, 6–95% duty-cycle tuning, under 50 µW). It deepened my interest in device-to-circuit co-design, and I am now extending that work toward spintronic memory and two-dimensional materials for neuromorphic hardware.

I am open to research opportunities and collaborations.
