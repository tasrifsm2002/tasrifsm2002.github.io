## Teaching interests

- **Device physics and materials:** Solid State Devices, Compound Semiconductor Devices, Semiconductor Physics, Optoelectronics, and Electrical Properties of Materials.
- **Circuits and systems:** VLSI Circuits and Design, Analog Integrated Circuit Design, Digital Electronics, and Microprocessor and Embedded Systems.

## Teaching philosophy

I want my students to understand why a circuit or a device behaves the way it does, not only how to compute an answer. In laboratory courses I try to connect every experiment back to the underlying theory, and to the practical question an engineer would ask: what do I expect to measure, and what does it mean when the result differs?

I believe teaching and research strengthen each other. Research keeps me close to open problems and to the tools of the field, such as circuit simulators, device models and measurement, and teaching forces me to explain those ideas clearly. I aim for a classroom where students are comfortable asking questions, learn by building and debugging, and leave with habits they can reuse: stating assumptions, checking results against theory, and documenting their work.
