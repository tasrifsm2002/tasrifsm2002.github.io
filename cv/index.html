  • 2025 - 2026

    Dhaka, Bangladesh

    Voltage Controlled High-Frequency Pulse Width Modulation Generation based on Spin Hall Nano-Oscillator
    BUET, Dept. of EEE — Undergraduate Thesis
    March 2025 – June 2026. Undergraduate thesis supervised by Dr. Orchi Hassan; defended June 2026.
    • Designed a hybrid spintronic-CMOS pulse-width-modulation (PWM) generator built around a field-free Spin Hall Nano-Oscillator (SHNO) driven by spin-orbit torque, replacing conventional high-speed CMOS oscillator cores.
    • Modeled and compared two circuit architectures: a 7T-1MTJ single-pFET design and a CMOS transmission-gate variant, using a macrospin LLGS magnetization-dynamics model for the MTJ alongside predictive FinFET device cards.
    • Achieved stable 7.5 GHz oscillation with 6-95% duty-cycle tunability at sub-50 microwatt power consumption (7T-1MTJ design).
    • The transmission-gate variant improves duty-cycle linearity (R² > 0.98 over a 20-85% range) with about 4× lower control sensitivity (~1.5%/mV versus ~6%/mV).
