*Control Systems Laboratory*

Implemented a closed-loop dual-axis solar tracker that computes real-time panel orientation from solar declination and hour-angle equations, using servo-based feedback driven by LDR sensors to continuously align the panel with the sun.

**Tools:** Arduino, servo actuators, LDR sensors
