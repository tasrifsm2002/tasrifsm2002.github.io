*Microprocessor & Embedded Systems Laboratory*

Built an autonomous line-following robot that interprets colour-coded commands in real time using three I2C-connected TCS34725 RGB sensors. Developed the full firmware stack on an STM32 (ARM Cortex-M) in C, including colour-classification logic and PWM-based motor control.

**Tools:** STM32 (ARM Cortex-M), C, I2C, TCS34725 RGB sensors
