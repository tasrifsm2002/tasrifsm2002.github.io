_Optoelectronics Laboratory_

Built a low-cost DIY spectrometer to track the daily spectral degradation of a TFT-LCD panel over 22 days, fitted first-order exponential decay models and quantified per-channel brightness loss across the RGB bands.

**Tools:** ESP32, Python, MATLAB, Webcam
