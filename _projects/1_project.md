*VLSI Laboratory*

Designed a fully synchronous 16-bit integer square-root unit using a shift-and-subtract algorithm with an 8-cycle latency and no multipliers. Took the design through the complete RTL-to-GDS flow — from Verilog/SystemVerilog RTL through synthesis, layered testbench verification, and physical implementation in Cadence Innovus.

**Tools:** Verilog, SystemVerilog, Cadence Virtuoso, Cadence Innovus
